# UAV Applications and Use Cases

## Introduction

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), commonly known as drones, have rapidly evolved from simple remote-controlled systems into highly intelligent platforms capable of performing complex autonomous tasks. This evolution has been driven by advances in sensing, localization, control, and artificial intelligence.

Today, UAVs are widely used across a diverse range of real-world applications, including agriculture, mapping, surveillance, emergency response, logistics, healthcare, and entertainment. These applications rely heavily on accurate localization and robust perception systems, which are enabled through a combination of sensors such as IMUs, GPS, cameras, LiDAR, and thermal imaging devices.

This repository provides a structured overview of major UAV applications, categorizing them based on real-world use cases. The goal is to offer a clear and organized reference for researchers, students, and engineers interested in understanding how UAV technology is applied in practice.

Each section describes a specific application domain, highlighting its objectives, operational context, and relevance to autonomous systems and sensor-based robotics.
